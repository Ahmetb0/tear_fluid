Notes

• This measures the detector against a human's judgment of what's a real particle on a handful of frames -- it is not a replacement for tracking accuracy (speed/radius over time), only for whether individual-frame detection is finding real particles and not spurious noise.

• Annotate frames spread across the recording (not just consecutive ones) and across a few different recordings/eyes if you can -- a handful of adjacent frames from one clip will under-represent how detection performs on your actual variability.

• Position error is only computed on matched pairs; it reflects how precisely the detector centers on a particle, not whether it found one.

• Your own clicks aren't perfectly precise either -- treat small position errors (a couple of px) as noise in the human annotation, not detector error.
